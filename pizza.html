Classic Cheese

The simplest and most basic type, featuring a tomato sauce base and a generous layer of melted cheese.

Pepperoni

A crowd favorite, topped with pepperoni slices, offering a spicy and savory flavor.

Supreme

Loaded with toppings like pepperoni, sausage, mushrooms, onions, and bell peppers.

Vegetarian

Perfect for those who avoid meat, featuring a variety of vegetables like mushrooms, onions, bell peppers, and olives.

BBQ Chicken

Topped with a tangy barbecue sauce, grilled chicken, and red onions.

Margherita

Inspired by the Italian flag, this pizza is topped with tomatoes, mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil leaves.

Buffalo Chicken

Spicy pizza topped with buffalo-style chicken, creamy ranch dressing, and sometimes blue cheese crumbles.
