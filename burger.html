Classic Cheeseburger

A foundational choice, featuring a beef patty topped with melted cheese, often American, and typically served on a sesame seed bun with lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, and special sauce.

Whopper

Burger King's signature burger, known for its flame-grilled beef patty, crisp lettuce, juicy tomato, creamy mayonnaise, crunchy pickles, and sliced white onions, all sandwiched between a soft sesame seed bun.

Big Mac

McDonald's iconic burger, composed of two all-beef patties, special sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles, and onions, nestled between three sesame seed buns.

Quarter Pounder

A hearty burger from McDonald's, featuring a quarter-pound of pure beef, served with cheese, pickles, onions, and mustard on a sesame seed bun.
