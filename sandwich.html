BLT

A classic combination of bacon, lettuce, and tomato, often served on toasted bread with mayonnaise.

Grilled Cheese

A simple yet satisfying sandwich made with cheese melted between two slices of bread, often grilled in butter.

Reuben

A classic deli sandwich featuring corned beef, Swiss cheese, sauerkraut, and Russian dressing on rye bread, often grilled.

Croque Monsieur

A French grilled ham and cheese sandwich, often topped with béchamel sauce and grated cheese.

Monte Cristo

A French-inspired sandwich featuring ham, cheese, and sometimes turkey, dipped in batter, fried, and dusted with powdered sugar.

Po' Boy

A New Orleans specialty, typically served on French bread with various fillings like fried seafood, roast beef, or other meats.
